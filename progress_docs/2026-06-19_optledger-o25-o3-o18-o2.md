## O2 — Sigil warm: mechanism prepared (scheduling is a GPU-budget decision)
Discovered the real gap: there is **no scheduled Sigil synthesis at all** — ~95% of rated entities
(7,760 / 8,108) have no `sigil_synthesis` row, so the Sigil card and the new O19 board serve `current:null`
for them. The literal finalize_fixture stage-4 hook (the ledger's O2) is risky (critical-function surgery
+ Gemma-storm potential) and narrower than the gap; the right fix is scheduling the existing, bounded
`vibesynth -mode nightly`. Added `scripts/hosting/cron-vibesynth.sh` (mirrors the sibling wrappers) with a
recommended crontab line in its header — **not installed**, because a nightly Gemma job is ~35 s/generation
(≈1-2 GPU-hours bounded by `-limit`) and draining the 7,760 backlog is multi-hour; that recurring GPU cost
is the owner's call. Live-tested `vibesynth -mode nightly -limit 2` (2 sigils generated, 0 failures).
