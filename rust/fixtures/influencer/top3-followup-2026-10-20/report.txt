READING
Qwen3.5 handled all six ordinary negative-emotion examples, versus three for OmniDimen and zero for Ministral. It still mistook low-intensity hope for mixed emotion, missed opposing coaches, and connected unrelated reporting to the target.
OmniDimen missed explicit confidence, injury worry, selection pressure and regret. Its emotional fine-tuning did not improve coverage over its parent model in this task.
Ministral often treated ordinary negative emotions as score 50 or abstained. Its faster median includes many short abstentions where another model supplied a reading.

FACTUAL PROSE
Correct scores do not imply faithful prose. Qwen3.5 added gendered pronouns in eight gender-unspecified synthetic cases; OmniDimen did so in three. All three invented a player affiliation in one real target-mismatch case. Other issues include invented roles, reasons, first names, speakers and confusing emotional change over time with contradiction. See manual-review.json for case IDs.

METHOD AND LIMITS
Same pinned Q4_K_M weights, llama.cpp build, 4096-token context, no thinking, temperature zero, seed 42, compact system prompt and production JSON schema as the previous final control. No prompt tuning between candidates. Latency covers warm completion prefill and decode, excludes load/template/application/DB overhead, and was measured with other local model services left resident.
The 23 real cases reuse 21 retained classifier diagnostics plus an explicit-confidence target counterfactual and an injury-concern headline. They are not a random or independent real-news holdout. Only four of the 19 hard-scored real cases contain target emotion; keep this small denominator in mind. Four cases involving dispute wording, nickname resolution or injury-concern interpretation receive qualitative review only. Missing publication dates stay null.
Labels are assistant-reviewed and provisional, not human gold. The matched suite follows a Qwen-only setup pilot that revealed an impossible basketball draw and an ambiguous relief target; both were corrected for every candidate, without changing the prompt. This is a prospective diagnostic comparison, not a blind holdout. No application routes, services or production records changed.
