ROLES
about contains zero to four exact phrases naming subjects or topics. Mentioned people do not automatically act or speak. Use [] when no named subject is needed.
speaker is an exact phrase naming the person or organization explicitly making a statement, announcement or quoted assessment; otherwise null. An opinion about a person is not that person's opinion. A team wanting a contract or winning a game need not have spoken. Do not fill an unidentified speaker from the publisher, a nearby name or general knowledge. If a headline has separate explicit speakers, keep their speech in separate statements. The publisher is already attached to the original source; you do not invent attribution.
kind is assertion, opinion, question or listing. It describes the selected clause, not a fact inferred from it. A possible recall phrased as a question stays question. An opinion or conditional prediction stays opinion. An actual page/show/watch/fixture heading is listing; a headline reporting an outcome is assertion. A how-to heading asks how to do something, not whether that thing is possible. Do not create a second generic listing just because the source is a headline.

QUALIFICATIONS AND UNRESOLVED EXPRESSIONS
qualifications contains up to five exact spans that limit the selected claim: conditions, doubt, contrasting assessments, negation or timing. Repeat these essential spans here if useful, but do not remove them from claim. Do not attach a return condition to another person's lay-off. A qualification copied correctly can still be assigned to the wrong statement.
unspecified contains up to four exact source expressions whose meaning, identity or required details the headline leaves unresolved. These are quoted expressions needing restraint, not prose explanations of what is missing. For an undefined amount, copy the amount expression; do not call it a fee or value. For an unexplained streak, copy the streak expression; do not invent its type. For unnamed requirements, copy the requirement expression. For an unnamed speaker or person, copy their original expression if one exists. A named actor does not have an unknown identity merely because you lack biographical details. Do not include incidental missing details, add a missing currency, or turn an absence into an injury. Use [] when no consequential expression needs flagging. An empty array does not mean every possible detail is known.
