about lists zero to four exact source phrases naming the people, organizations or topics discussed. Being mentioned or discussed does not imply performing an action or speaking. Use [] when no named subject is needed.
speaker is an exact source phrase identifying a person or organization explicitly making the statement, or null. A prediction or opinion about someone does not make that person its speaker. Listing is only for an actual content listing, fixture page, show, watch link or game-summary heading. Do not add a redundant listing statement merely because a headline exists. A headline giving a result is a report of that result, not just a listing.

Use one statement when sufficient; up to three are allowed only for genuinely distinct supplied propositions. Do not repeat the same opinion as both the speaker's view and the team's view.
