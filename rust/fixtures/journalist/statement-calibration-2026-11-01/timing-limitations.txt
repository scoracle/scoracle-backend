Measurements are local request-to-result timings, not an isolated hardware benchmark. No compiler or other experiment was launched concurrently with inference. A mid-run read-only CPU snapshot showed ordinary system/application activity, including PerfPowerServices around 107% CPU; no competing inference run was identified. Shared machine load and different cache-prefix reuse affect these timings. No system process was stopped. Manual grouping, review, production scheduling, retries and mixed-character workload are excluded. No daily-capacity extrapolation is justified.
