# Next milestone: reliable comparative Scout readings

Checkpoint: September 14, 2026. The shared context implementation is complete
locally; editorial quality and end-to-end database execution remain unqualified.

The product goal is a supported explanation of how contribution changes across
teams and seasons. A provider-to-scorer transition is one possible finding;
continued strength in both roles, no meaningful change and insufficient evidence
are equally valid. Memory supplies the comparison, and the character interprets it.

They do not certify the newly wired production adapter or its complete request
once current evidence, voice and output reservation are included.

## Make the comparison readable before tuning the voice again

Inspect which administrative timestamps, calendar fields and source envelopes
still reach the writer. Keep a complete audit package, while presenting only the
dates and facts needed for the sporting interpretation. Observation dates must
remain distinguishable from signing dates, and reporting periods from fixtures.
Keep the named historical team, matching measurement identities, denominators,
uncertainty and material conflicts together.

Use the existing Scout character, form and route as the control. Change one input
presentation at a time. The rejected s34 experiment showed that a voice-only edit
did not resolve temporal errors, unsupported causes or recitation of metadata.
Avoid adding outcome-specific wording, keyword targets or speculative role labels.

## Evidence needed to proceed

Review a small set covering a supported change in contribution, a stable profile,
a thin sample and a disputed affiliation. Include current news in the actual
production request. Require:

1. Every comparison uses compatible measures, competition and time bases.
2. No invented dates, missing-as-zero values, or inference of actual playing time
   from source coverage. Unsupported role changes remain unresolved.
3. The reading explains sporting significance rather than reciting the package.
4. Complete output fits the surface and the full provider context window.
5. Corrected source facts change the generation key; repeated observations or
   a new prior card alone do not produce a refresh loop.

Review individual claims against retained sources, not only parser acceptance.
Keep raw failures. Once the Scout comparison is reliable, repeat this complete
request check for the Journalist and Insider before broadening the other voices.
