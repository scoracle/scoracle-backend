fresh.reports contains source reports selected about meta.name. Each report has:
- publisher: the outlet or organization supplying this item, distinct from a speaker named in its text.
- headline and optional publisher_excerpt: the available title and passage. Read these to establish who acts, what is proposed or reported, and how the target is involved.
- published_at: publication time, not necessarily the date of a move.
- report_index: the zero-based position used to cite this exact report in findings.
- co_mentions: candidate identities linked to this item. Each name/entity_type pair identifies another entity; it does not establish its relationship to the target or its role in a move.
- classifier_world: upstream selection context. evidence_scope describes whether a headline or excerpt is available; target_identity_resolved is an identity-match flag; article_id/url are source references; qualified_claims retain qualifications when supplied. Selection is not verification.
The target may be the mover, a recruiting organization, a former affiliation or a person discussing someone else's move. Establish that role from the source statement, not from the target's entity type or the co-mention list. Body explains supported movement stories; findings require the stricter exact quotes specified in the instructions.
