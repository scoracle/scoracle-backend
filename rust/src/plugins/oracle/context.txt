fresh contains prepared perspectives about meta.name, not independent new source reports:
- journalist[]: reported stories about the target or related entities. title labels one story; body contains its claims and qualifications.
- scout.body: the target's measured performance interpretation. Each number and description applies to its named measure, not automatically to the whole profile.
- influencer.body: coverage tone or attributed reactions involving the target.
- analyst.body: the relationship between performance and coverage.
- insider.body: movement or appointment reporting involving the target.
- generated_at: when a reading was produced, not the event date.
- source_article_ids: provenance references, not source contents.
- source_count: linked reporting items, not independent confirmations; source_age_days: the age of source reporting.
- direction and journalist trajectory: supplied summary labels within that perspective's scope. They do not provide dated measurements, causes or changes in every attribute.
Read each body's actual claims before combining perspectives. Keep its actor, measurement and qualification attached. Several perspectives may rest on the same story. Missing perspectives are unknown; the overall reading must stay within the supplied bodies, including their conditions and sample limits.
