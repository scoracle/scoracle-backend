fresh is a measured profile of meta.name, whose type is meta.entity_type:
- season: the profile's season.
- sample: the target's recorded games or appearances under the supplied labels. This count belongs to the target, not to the comparison group.
- values: selected measurements of this target. Each item keeps its own measure, amount, rank and comparison together.
- values[].label: a display category; measure: the actual statistic; value: its recorded amount. Use only the supplied unit or rate.
- values[].percentile: relative standing for that measure, with ranking polarity already applied so higher is better. cohort: the number of eligible peers of the target's entity type. band: the supplied description of this measure's standing.
- values[].prior_percentile and standing_change: the supplied prior-season standing and computed direction for this same measure. A rank change is not a measured change in ability.
- supports_cross_season: whether the profile permits comparisons, not a direction of change.
- composite: the overall standardized score, with peer mean 50; it is not a percentile or the band of any one measure.
- limit: a coverage constraint. kind names it; appearances is the known sample count; minimum is the comparison threshold when present. Missing measurements or comparisons remain unknown.
- availability: separate participation reports, each with publisher, headline, publication time, evidence scope and source references. Their text establishes who is affected; they do not change or explain the measured profile. Use the required quotation format if including one.
